User Guides

Short, printable walkthroughs for the DeJAVU volunteer portal on Better Impact. Open a guide and use Print / Save as PDF when you need a handout.

Live portal: volunteers.ja-africa.org

  • Set Up a Volunteer Account

    Apply to volunteer from the DeJAVU homepage and create your MyImpactPage account.

    Audience · New volunteer

  • Complete Safeguarding

    Read the policy, confirm the declaration, and submit so you can access opportunities.

    Audience · New volunteer

  • Log In to Your Account

    Sign in from the DeJAVU homepage and open your volunteer home page.

    Audience · Volunteer

  • Complete the Skills Survey

    From My Profile → Additional Info, rate your skills and choose areas to support.

    Audience · Volunteer

  • Apply for Opportunities

    After Safeguarding, browse the Opportunities list, sign up for a shift, and confirm.

    Audience · Volunteer

  • Log Your Hours

    Record time against an activity under Hours, then check Most Recent Entries.

    Audience · Volunteer
