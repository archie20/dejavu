Participation Tracking & Feedback Architecture

Operational Brief

DeJAVU has been configured to use auto-approval for hours. That removes administrative approval bottlenecks and allows us gather feedback for Monitoring & Evaluation (M&E).

⚙️

Platform Mechanics

  • Auto-approved hours Logged time immediately updates progress toward the 80% completion rule required for M&E success and digital certificates.
  • Communication constraint Better Impact does not natively trigger automated emails when a volunteer completes their opportunity.
⚡

Challenge

  • Survey fatigue Prompting for feedback on every routine shift causes frustration and rushed responses.
  • The objective Keep routine hour logging under a few seconds while capturing clean feedback at program completion.
📌
Purpose of this guide: The four options below are practical ways to align feedback collection with the opportunity lifecycle — balancing volunteer experience, admin effort, and implementation timelines.
