PHASE 2

Vetting & Qualification Gating

When a volunteer registers or self-schedules, the system evaluates active gate requirements against their profile credentials.

PHASE 3

Acceptance Pathway Routing

Once gates pass, the volunteer is routed through one of two configured acceptance pathways.

📋 Pathway 1 — Manual ROC/Staff Review

Phase 1 baseline. Admin verifies profile before assignment.

  1. 1
    Volunteer Applies Registration submitted for opportunity
  2. 2
    Pending Review (In-Process) Awaiting Admin
  3. 3
    Alert Triggered Spoke / ROC Admin notified
  4. 4
    Admin Accept / Assign Manual profile verification
  5. 5
    Scheduled Volunteer Assigned
OR

⚡ Pathway 2 — Auto-Assign (Self-Scheduling)

Bypasses admin queue when all qualifications are verified.

  1. 1
    Volunteer Clicks Sign Up Selects open opportunity shift
  2. 2
    System Evaluates Badges All required qualifications checked
  3. 3
    All Qualifications True? Instant routing decision
  4. 4
    Scheduled — Instantly No Admin Queue
If any qualification is False: Registration blocked — volunteer required to complete missing credentials.
PHASES 4 & 5

Delivery, Hour Logging & M&E Validation

After offline sessions are delivered and approved hours are logged, the system evaluates participation against the MERL team's 80% completion rule.

≥ 80%

Scenario A — Successful Participant

Volunteer met or exceeded the committed hour threshold.

MERL Completion Rule
Completion % = (Approved Hours ÷ Committed Hours) × 100
Committed Hours set at opportunity creation (Phase 1)
< 80%

Scenario B — Incomplete Participation

Volunteer did not meet the minimum hour threshold.
